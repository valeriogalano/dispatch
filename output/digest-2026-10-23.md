## Botcaster

### Fixed
- `dc128ef` fix: report a kick only when Telegram accepts the ban
  The cron deleted the pending key and notified "kicked" whatever banChatMember answered, so a refused ban left the user muted in the group with nothing left to retry it. A failed ban now restores the key, the next tick retries, and the admin gets a one-time failure notice. The key is also deleted before the kick rather than after, so a bot rejoining mid-kick keeps its fresh challenge.
