## Podcast Audiogram Generator
<https://github.com/valeriogalano/podcast-audiogram-generator>

### Fixed
- [`8c65bd8`](https://github.com/valeriogalano/podcast-audiogram-generator/commit/8c65bd8ed9040cb94046d53a75c34332abccb8d1) fix(rss): use itunes:episode as episode identity with positional fallback (#4)
  services/rss.py numbered episodes by their feed position (idx+1),
  ignoring the itunes:episode tag. The two numberings coincide today only
  by convention (150 items <-> ep1..150); a trailer/bonus item, a capped
  feed or a removed episode would silently shift every id (release names,
  published.json, captions).
  
  - parse_feed: take number from itunes:episode when present, falling back
    to the 1-based feed position (oldest->newest) when absent or non-numeric.
  - cli: derive max_episode from the highest episode number instead of
    len(episodes), so --episode N stays valid with a capped/shifted feed.
  - tests: itunes:episode wins over position; an extra untagged item does
    not shift ep150's identity; non-numeric itunes:episode falls back.
- [`20ff8d7`](https://github.com/valeriogalano/podcast-audiogram-generator/commit/20ff8d716f6f4b04a41ca60ce8d0e44054849c84) fix(cli): exit with non-zero status on config, feed or rendering errors (#3)
  main() loggava gli errori di config/feed (feed_url, episode, soundbites
  mancanti, feed vuoto) e proseguiva con un `return` implicito, e le
  eccezioni di rendering in _process_single_soundbite/_process_full_episode
  venivano catturate e ignorate in future.result(). In entrambi i casi il
  processo terminava con exit 0: un run CI fallito risultava verde.
  
  _process_single_soundbite ora rilancia se un formato fallisce il
  rendering; _render_soundbites_batch e _process_full_episode propagano
  il fallimento come bool; process_one_episode e main() aggregano lo
  stato e restituiscono un exit code non zero. __main__.py ora chiama
  sys.exit(main()), altrimenti il valore di ritorno non avrebbe alcun
  effetto sul codice di uscita del processo.
