## Timebox
<https://github.com/valeriogalano/Timebox>

### Added
- [`20d8bb7`](https://github.com/valeriogalano/Timebox/commit/20d8bb717340eeda6b8943e8b47c8929a5f998bf) feat: show area limits in Andamento and split weekly limits from total budgets
  The Settimana lens only listed project budgets, under a subtitle saying
  "independent of the period" even though the weekly project limit does depend
  on it. Split the section in two, by what the cap means:
  
  - Limiti della settimana: weekly area limits and weekly project hours,
    against the hours tracked in the selected week.
  - Budget totali: global area limits and total project budgets, against
    all hours since the start.
  
  Each card now shows a single bar. Areas with a weekly limit also get a
  Limite column in the per-area consuntivo, flagged when exceeded.
- [`862d972`](https://github.com/valeriogalano/Timebox/commit/862d9729a3860da4b8a5def88d4f0a67714de321) feat: show the active-area glyph next to minimal and closed ones
  The glyph for the active state already existed but was only drawn in the
  legend; the weekly cells, the project label and the recurring rows skipped
  it explicitly. Drop the 'active' exclusion so every area carries its state.

### Fixed
- [`80d9de0`](https://github.com/valeriogalano/Timebox/commit/80d9de0684512464444484bdba58e1a6027bfae9) fix: count archived projects in the consumed hours of a global area limit
  The Budget totali card for an area summed only active projects, while the
  In prospettiva lens sums all of them. Hours spent on archived projects were
  spent against the same cap, so both views now agree.
- [`6fe9ec1`](https://github.com/valeriogalano/Timebox/commit/6fe9ec15c0dfc1b55a225f43d7c215272d531d7b) fix: scale weekly block height like the recurring view
  Weekly blocks used 30px/h with a 46px floor, so everything up to 1.5h got
  the same height, while the recurring view is proportional at 64px/h. Use the
  same scale and the same 0.5h floor.
  
  Blocks shorter than 46px cannot stack name, duration and progress bar, so the
  duration moves into the header next to the name and the bar stays at the
  bottom. The compact variant scales proportionally with a 30px floor.
