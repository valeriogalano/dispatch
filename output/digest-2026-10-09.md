## Timebox

### Added
- [`f41ad0b`](https://github.com/valeriogalano/Timebox/commit/f41ad0bdecf6d5b526d25ce370ec573de673aaf0) feat: open the mobile page on any network, with an always-visible switch
  The home network could not be recognised from inside the app: on macOS the ARP table comes back empty when a non-Apple program is in the process chain, so the router's MAC address is unreadable (it only worked from a terminal), and neither the router's UPnP id nor its TLS certificate identified it. The page is now reachable on whatever local network the computer is on while it is switched on. Since nothing closes it automatically, the switch moves to the top bar, visible from every screen; Settings keeps the link and the token. lib/home-network.js only finds the addresses to bind to.
- [`f3c0c86`](https://github.com/valeriogalano/Timebox/commit/f3c0c86147412686eeca4243affb18bee3d3d7ac) feat: log hours from the iPhone on the home network
  A second HTTP listener (port 37374, macOS only, off by default) serves a small page for logging worked hours from a phone. It is separate from the loopback API, which has no authentication and stays on 127.0.0.1.
  
  - lib/mobile-server.js: four static files and two routes behind a bearer token (GET /api/day, PUT /api/hours), with a CSP that only allows the page's own files.
  - lib/mobile-access.js: the listener is open only when enabled in Settings, a token exists, a home network is declared and the router reached right now is that one. It rechecks every 30 seconds, closes on any doubt and binds to the addresses in the router's subnet only.
  - lib/home-network.js: recognises the network by the router's MAC address, since macOS hides the Wi-Fi name without the location permission.
  - cli/commands/mobile.js: day data and save by project id. Only worked hours change; stored billable hours and billed state are kept.
  - The save rule DayScreen used inline is now planDayEntrySave, shared with the page through a CommonJS twin.
  - Settings gets an iPhone section; SECURITY.md documents the accepted risks (plain HTTP on the home network, MAC spoofing).
- [`d6ea889`](https://github.com/valeriogalano/Timebox/commit/d6ea889df5283ef6a1d87d8ecd3ca75576f54615) feat: keep slots on the plan only, not on tracked hours
  The slot stored on an entry is picked automatically and cannot be chosen, so it says nothing about when the work happened. Tracked hours now meet slots only through fillPlannedBlocks, which spreads an area's hours for the day over its blocks in slot order. The per-slot capacity bars, the Giorno diagnostics computed in the main process (free capacity, ready blocks, mismatches) and day_summary use that fill instead of the entry's slot. today lists the day's entries without slots, Rendiconto no longer shows the slot, and the standalone week table drops its AM/PM columns.
- [`be64033`](https://github.com/valeriogalano/Timebox/commit/be64033a0cc87afd1b37964029dc214c708ca7bb) feat: make hour cells, selectors and help reachable from the keyboard
  Hour cells take focus and open on Enter or Space; Escape hands focus back to the cell. The segmented selectors in Settimana and the Andamento lenses are buttons, and the area list in Aree is focusable. HelpDot moves to a shared component that opens its text on focus as well as on hover, replacing the native title that only the mouse could reach; the weekly breakdown in Da decidere uses it too.
- [`b6c18ee`](https://github.com/valeriogalano/Timebox/commit/b6c18ee86119dd8b44a56abd5f4707fd111a039f) feat: expose the Registro entries over MCP, CLI and HTTP
  GET /entries returns the single entries of a date range, filtered by area and project, with worked and billable totals by area and by project. The MCP tool 'entries' and 'timebox entries' sit on top of it. Billable hours and the billed flag are reported only for hourly areas, as everywhere else.
- [`e8b4c62`](https://github.com/valeriogalano/Timebox/commit/e8b4c6239124c8246cd1c7405d4353ad685a4abe) feat: redesign the "Da decidere" card around the decision it asks for
  The card was three lines of small grey text: a triangle that read as an
  expand control, the average in the area colour (a red or green number
  looked like a verdict), and the action as the faintest line.
  
  Now the verdict is written out, the average sits next to a bar against
  the plan with its percentage, one mini bar per closed week shows the
  distribution without the tooltip, and the worst week carries its date.
  The action comes last and states the value: the measured average rounded
  to the quarter hour, against today's recurrence when it differs. Areas
  over plan point to the recurrence too, not to Settimana.
- [`4642a18`](https://github.com/valeriogalano/Timebox/commit/4642a18aac997cef63b9b7c41525c460d6404f2f) feat: measure limits on both worked and billable hours in hourly areas
  A cap in an hourly area can mean time spent or hours sold, so it is now measured on both counts. When they differ, the Andamento cards show both with their label, and bars, percentages and alerts follow the worse one and say which. Applies to the Settimana banners and counters, the block meters in Settimana and Giorno, In prospettiva, and the status alerts in CLI and MCP. Fixed-fee and unpaid areas keep counting worked hours only.
- [`874c4f6`](https://github.com/valeriogalano/Timebox/commit/874c4f6df23368586aadea9e7d7bc6917e12ff34) feat: limit Rendiconto to hourly areas

- [`4ab6bb2`](https://github.com/valeriogalano/Timebox/commit/4ab6bb255d21b0b02fb8e91a0d614ef8ca6c1088) feat: show remaining hours on the limit cards in Andamento
